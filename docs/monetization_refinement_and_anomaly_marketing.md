### 2.3 VIP Conversion Strategy

**When to show the VIP prompt:**
1. **After 3rd dive** — "You've explored 3 zones! Unlock VIP for exclusive perks"
2. **On reaching 1,000m depth** — "You've reached the Twilight Zone! VIP helps you go deeper"
3. **After first rare catch** — "That rare creature is worth 50% more with VIP Credits!"

**Note:** These are popups, not forced screens. Max 3 lifetime prompts. Roblox players hate aggressive monetization.

## 4. Anomaly Marketing — Our #1 Competitive Advantage

### 4.1 Why Anomaly is Viral

From the Competitor Feature Audit: **"Zero competitors have an anomaly system."** Our anomaly system is the ONLY feature in the ocean/fishing genre that no other game has. This is our marketing goldmine.

**The Anomaly Hook:** "What happens when the ocean fights back?"

### 4.2 Five Anomaly Event Types — Marketing Angles

| Anomaly | Visual Effect | Marketing Hook | TikTok Angle |
|---------|--------------|----------------|-------------|
| **Bioluminescent Bloom** | Neon pink/blue water, glowing creatures | "The ocean is GLOWING and I don't know why 🌊✨" | Satisfying neon visuals, ASMR |
| **Corrupted Waters** | Blood-red water, aggressive creatures | "The water turned BLOOD RED. I kept diving." | Horror, suspense, "scary" |
| **Abyssal Storm** | Whirlpool, turbulence, reduced visibility | "A STORM is forming in the deep... do you dive or flee?" | High stakes, thrill |
| **Echo Event** | Ghostly outlines of giant creatures | "I saw something that shouldn't exist down there 👁️" | Mystery, "what was that?" |
| **Mega Migration** | Hundreds of creatures moving together | "THE ENTIRE OCEAN IS MOVING. Follow them." | Spectacle, FOMO |

### 4.3 TikTok Campaign — "The Anomaly Challenge"

**Format:** Users record themselves encountering an anomaly event and reacting.

**Template 1: "The Scariest Dive"**
```
[Hook 0:00-0:03] "I dove 4,000 meters in Roblox and REGRETTED it"
[Build 0:03-0:10] Normal gameplay → water starts turning red → strange sounds
[Payoff 0:10-0:15] GIANT CREATURE EMERGES FROM THE DARK
[CTA 0:15] "Play Abyss of the Deep. Don't go alone."
```

**Template 2: "Satisfying Glowing Ocean"**
```

[Show 0:02-0:10] Bioluminescent bloom — neon particles everywhere
[Text overlay] "This happens randomly during dives"
```

**Template 3: "What's That Sound?"**
```

[Show 0:03-0:12] Player looking around, flashlight in dark water

[CTA 0:15] "What did you find in the deep? Comment below 👇"
```
