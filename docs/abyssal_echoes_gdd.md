# Game Design Document: Abyssal Echoes

**Revision:** 1.0
**Author:** Agent Roblox Game Designer
**Status:** Initial Draft

---

## 1. Executive Summary
**Abyssal Echoes** is a deep-sea exploration and survival experience on Roblox. Players descend through increasingly dangerous ocean layers, collect rare bioluminescent creatures, manage limited resources, and build underwater sanctuaries. The game leverages the current "Anomaly" trend by introducing unpredictable environmental shifts and mysterious entities in the deepest zones.

## 2. Core Gameplay Loop
The loop follows a standard but highly polished simulator progression:
1.  **Descend:** Dive into the ocean using diving suits or submarines. Higher pressure resistance (upgrades) is required for deeper zones.
2.  **Explore & Harvest:** Find rare minerals, scan bioluminescent flora, and catch exotic fish.
3.  **Process & Sell:** Return to a "Research Station" (base or hub) to convert findings into **Depth Credits** and **Research Points**.
4.  **Upgrade:** Spend Credits/Points on:
    *   **Suits:** Oxygen capacity, Pressure resistance, Movement speed.
    *   **Submarines:** Hull strength, Cargo space, Lighting range, Battery life.
    *   **Tools:** Better harpoons, scanners, and mining drills.

## 3. Theme & Art Direction
*   **Primary Theme:** Deep-Sea Sci-Fi / Atmospheric Mystery (Leaning towards "Atmospheric Stylized").
*   **Visuals:** High-contrast lighting. Dark environments illuminated by player-controlled lights and vibrant bioluminescent life.
*   **Sound:** Immersive underwater ambiance, muffled sonar pings, and eerie "echo" sounds when anomalies are near.

## 4. Progression Milestones (The Depth Zones)
Progression is gated by **Pressure Resistance** levels.

| Zone | Depth | Description | Key Unlocks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sunlight** | 0m - 200m | Bright, coral reefs, friendly fish. Tutorial area. | Basic Submarine |
| **Twilight** | 200m - 1000m | Dim light, first bioluminescent plants. Predatory sharks. | Submarine Turret |
| **Midnight** | 1000m - 4000m | Pitch black. High pressure. Rare crystals. First "Anomalies". | Base Building Pod |
| **The Abyss** | 4000m - 6000m | Extreme pressure. Giant squids. Ancient ruins. | Heavy Sub-Crawler |
| **The Trench** | 6000m+ | End-game. High-tier anomalies. Most valuable resources. | "The Beacon" |

## 5. The 'Hook' (What makes it unique?)
*   **The Anomaly System:** Unlike static simulators, Abyssal Echoes features "Echo Events". Randomly, a zone can become "Corrupted" or "Enchanted," changing fish behavior, resource spawns, and visual effects (e.g., water turning blood red or freezing).
*   **Base Building in the Deep:** Players can construct modular underwater bases. These act as spawn points, storage, and defense hubs against deep-sea creatures during Anomaly events.
*   **Hybrid Gameplay:** Combines the chill "Collection" aspect of simulators with the tension of "Survival/Horror" (Anomaly).
*   **The Abyssal Journal (Social/Educational):** A shareable bestiary where players document caught species and anomalies. Includes 'educational' facts (Species Identification) to appeal to the parent-approved "educational" trend seen in top typing games.

## 6. Initial MVP Scope (Minimum Viable Product)
*   **Map:** A vertical play space including the Sunlight and Twilight zones.
*   **Core Systems:** 
    *   Diving mechanic (Oxygen/Pressure UI).
    *   Simple harvesting (Click-to-collect resources).
    *   The Research Hub (Selling/Upgrading).
    *   Two Submarine types (Starter and Mid-tier).
*   **Art:** Basic bioluminescent effects and 5-10 fish species.
*   **Anomaly:** One basic "Echo Event" that changes lighting and spawns a "Phantom" predator.

## 7. Monetization & Live-Ops Strategy
*   **The Depth Pass (Seasonal):** A battle-pass style progression system where players earn exclusive skins and tools by completing weekly deep-sea challenges. (Aligned with RIVALS' high-monetization model).
*   **Game Passes:** 
    *   "Infinite Oxygen" (Convenience).
    *   "Deep Sea Scanner" (P2W - reveals rare resources).
    *   "Exclusive Submarine Skins".
*   **Developer Products:** 
    *   Instant Oxygen refills.
    *   Bait Packs (guarantees rare fish spawn).
    *   Currency Bundles (Depth Credits).
*   **Weekly Content Treadmill:** Commitment to weekly "New Species" or "New Submarine Part" drops to maintain chart visibility ([UPD] tags).
*   **Premium Payouts:** Encouraged through high session duration (deep exploration takes time).
