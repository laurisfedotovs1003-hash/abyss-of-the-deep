## 4. Pricing Psychology & Strategy

### 4.1 The "Goldilocks" Tier Strategy

Developer Products use a 3-tier pricing model proven by mobile games:

| Tier | Price | Purpose | Conversion Target |
|------|-------|---------|-------------------|
| **Small** (₡500 = 49 R$) | Under $1 USD | Impulse buy, removes friction | 60% of purchases |
| **Medium** (₡2,000 = 149 R$) | ~$2 USD | Considered purchase, "fair price" | 25% of purchases |
| **Large** (₡10,000 = 499 R$) | ~$6 USD | Whale bait, best value | 15% of purchases |

**Why it works:** The large pack offers 2x the value-per-R$ of the small pack, creating the "I should buy the big one" illusion. Most players buy small, but whales drive 80% of revenue on large.

### 4.2 Starter Pack Psychology

The Starter Pack (99 R$) should be positioned as:
- **"Limited time"** — displayed with a countdown or "New Player Offer" badge
- **Highest value** — ~500 R$ value for 99 R$ (5x value perception)
- **One per account** — creates urgency

**Comparable:** Adopt Me! "Starter Bundle" at 99 R$ is one of their most-purchased items.

### 4.3 VIP Status — The Recurring Revenue Engine

VIP (249 R$) should offer **permanent** benefits (not monthly subscription):
- **Why permanent:** Roblox does not natively support subscriptions well, and the 9-16 demographic has inconsistent payment methods
- **How it converts:** After a player has spent 2-3 hours in game, show a popup: "You've completed 10 dives! Unlock VIP for permanent bonuses"
- **Bonuses:** Keep as-is (+25% Credits, +15% XP, +5 max O₂, VIP title)

## 6. Launch Marketing Plan

### 6.1 Roblox Organic Discovery (Primary Channel — 80% of initial traffic)

Roblox's algorithm rewards **session time** and **engagement rate** over installs. Our strategy:

#### Pre-Launch (2 weeks before)

| Action | Why | Owner |
|--------|-----|-------|
| Set up **game icon** with glowing "🔦" emoji and dark background | High contrast stands out in grid | Tech Artist |
| Write description with "swipe-able" bullet points for mobile | Mobile users read first 3 lines only | Lead |
| Tag game as **"Adventure"** + **"RPG"** + **"Horror"** | Horror tag catches Anomaly audience | Market Analyst |
| Add 5 thumbnail screenshots: Bright reef → Dark trench progression | Visual progression hooks curiosity | Tech Artist |
| Set up **6 game servers** at launch (scale up on demand) | Prevents "dead server" first impression | Lead Developer |

#### Launch Day

| Action | Why |
|--------|-----|
| **Soft launch at 3 PM EST** (peak Roblox traffic) | Catches after-school + evening surge |
| First hour: **5 DevForum posts** (WIP, Showcase, Updates, Resources, Collaboration) | DevForum = free visibility to 2M+ creators |
| **Spawn 20 "creatures" near spawn** in first zone | First 60 seconds must be visually overwhelming |
| Force first-time players into **tutorial dive** immediately (no menu) | "Begin play" > "Read instructions" |

#### Week 1 — Algorithm Hacks

| Tactic | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Session length optimization** | Oxygen economy keeps players diving 15-25 min (bliss point) |
| **Floor loot psychology** | Every first dive of the session guarantees a "Rare" or better creature |
| **"[UPD]" in title on Day 3** | Push a small content update (2 new creatures) to trigger algorithm re-evaluation |
| **Weekend event:** "Double Credits Weekend" | Creates social urgency — "play now before it's over" |

## 10. Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| **Priced too high** | Medium | Medium | Start at recommended prices, monitor conversion. If <2% paying, run 24hr 50% off sale and measure elasticity |
| **Priced too low** | Low | Low | Easy to raise prices; whales buy regardless |
| **No paying users week 1** | Low | High | Give free "Depth Pass trial" to first 1,000 players to establish paying behavior |
| **TikTok strategy flops** | Medium | Low | Organic only — $0 budget at risk. Pivot to YouTube walkthroughs |
| **Roblox algorithm doesn't pick up** | Medium | High | Run "[UPD]" updates weekly — each update resets algorithm consideration |
